# Quickstart: Remove Book Suggestions

1. Sign in as a regular member while the club is open for book suggestions.
2. Suggest a title if that member has none. Remove is beside that title and is absent beside a title suggested by someone else.
3. Remove the member's own title. It leaves the list. Suggesting that same title again succeeds.
4. Sign in as bow with the password admin. Remove is beside every title. Remove a title suggested by someone else. It leaves the list for the regular member too.
