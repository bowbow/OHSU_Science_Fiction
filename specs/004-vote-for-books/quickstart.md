# Quickstart: Vote for a Book

1. Sign in while the club is open for book suggestions and at least two books are listed. Each shows 0 votes, with the earlier suggestion first.
2. Vote for the lower book. It shows 1 vote and moves to the top. It says "Your vote".
3. Vote for the other book. The first book returns to 0 votes. The second shows 1 vote, says "Your vote", and is at the top.
4. Sign in as a different member and vote for the same book. Its count becomes 2.
