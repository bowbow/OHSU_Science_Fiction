# Quickstart: Open Book Suggestions

1. Sign in as bow with the password admin.
2. Open Book club. Open suggestions. The list is empty.
3. Sign out. Register a new member, or sign in as a regular member.
4. Open Book club and suggest a title. The list shows that title. Pointing at the title shows the member's username.
5. Sign out and sign in again. The same title is still listed.
6. Suggest the same title in another letter case. The page says it is already on the list.
7. Sign in as bow. The book club state is "Open for book suggestions", the earlier title is still listed, and bow can add another title to that same list.
